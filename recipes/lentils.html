Lentils

Description

This one is easy to make and very good on the macros.

Ingredients

  • Lentils
  • 1 Teaspoon Tomato Sauce

Steps

  1. Wash the debris away from the lentils
  2. Set-up a boiling water
  3. Put the lentils and the tomato sauce into the pot.
  4. Pour the boiling water into the pot. Pour until the lentils are hard to see.
  5. Cook it at low heat for 40 minutes.
