Omlette

Description

A strong start for the morning.

Ingredients:

  • 3 Whole Eggs
  • 30g of cheese
  • 1.5 Tablespoon Olive Oil

Steps

  1. Pour the cracked eggs and olive oil into a non-stick pan.
  2. Cook it at low heat until a semi-solid structure is formed. Grate the cheese in the meantime
  3. Add the grated cheese and cook until desired visuals are reached.
