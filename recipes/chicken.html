Chicken

Description

This baby will keep you full for the whole day. It is unbearable without any extras.

Ingredients:

  • Chicken Breast
  • 1 Tablespoon Salt
  • 1.5 Tablespoon Olive Oil
  • Spices to Your Hearts Content

Steps

  1. Heat up the air fryer at 160 celcius for 5 minutes
  2. Coat the chicken in olive oil, salt and spices
  3. Place the chicken inside the air fryer and make small cuts on its surface for equal heating
  4. Cook it for 26 minutes on 160 celcius, flip it at 13 minute mark
